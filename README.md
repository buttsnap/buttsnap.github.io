## What is ButtSnap?

ButtSnap turns your everyday moments into beautiful stickers. See a cute cup at the café, a tiny flower on the sidewalk, or a cool keychain at a shop? Just point your camera, snap, and watch it become a lovely collectible sticker — complete with a soft white border and a gentle glow.

No accounts. No uploads. Everything happens right on your phone.

## ✨ Features

- **Snap to Sticker** — Photograph anything around you and the app automatically creates a polished sticker
- **Daily Sticker Diary** — All your stickers are neatly organized by date, like a visual journal
- **Themed Collections** — Group stickers into collections like "City Walk", "Coffee Cups", or "Cute Cars"
- **Creative Canvas** — Combine stickers into collages and sticker walls, then share them with friends
- **100% Private** — Everything happens on your device. No photos are ever uploaded anywhere. No account needed.
