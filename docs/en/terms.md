## 2. Service Description

ButtSnap is a sticker-collection app that lets you photograph everyday objects and moments, then automatically turns them into collectible stickers. Core features include:

- Snap-to-sticker: Photograph a subject and have it automatically isolated and styled as a sticker
- Date-organized sticker timeline for browsing your collection
- Themed collections for curating and grouping stickers
- Canvas for creating collages and sticker walls
- Data export and import for full control over your collection

ButtSnap Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription that unlocks unlimited daily captures and premium backgrounds. The subscription is managed entirely through your Apple ID.
